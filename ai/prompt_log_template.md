# Prompt Log Template

## Task

Briefly describe the task or problem.

## Prompt / Question to AI

Record the important prompt or a concise summary of what was asked.

## AI Suggestion / Output

Summarise the relevant AI response.

## What I Checked

Explain how the suggestion was tested against the code, data, outputs or assignment requirements.

## What Was Wrong or Incomplete

Record any issue, missing detail, incorrect assumption or overclaim.

## What I Changed

Explain the correction or decision made.

## Why

Explain the methodological, technical or economic reason for the change.

## Final Outcome

Record the final implemented result.

## Reflection

State what this episode showed about appropriate AI use.
